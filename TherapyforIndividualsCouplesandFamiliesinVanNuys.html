Meet the Center

About New U Therapy Center & Family Service

We are a Van Nuys therapy center that welcomes individuals, couples and families, with the belief that relationships and mental health shape one another.

Our services include mental health therapy, anxiety and depression treatment, ADHD behavioral therapy, trauma and PTSD counseling with EMDR, and psychiatric medication management for adults.

We focus on personalized plans and holistic emotional wellness, which means looking at stress, habits and relationships alongside symptoms.

The center is open Monday to Saturday from 9 am to 7 pm. Ask us about virtual appointments when you call.

A Family-Minded Therapy Center in Van Nuys

New U Therapy Center & Family Service is a counseling and psychiatric practice serving Van Nuys and nearby communities in the San Fernando Valley. We see adults on their own, couples working on their relationship and families navigating stress together.

Our style is warm, practical and collaborative. We aim to make therapy approachable, explain what each type of session involves and build a personalized plan that evolves with you.
